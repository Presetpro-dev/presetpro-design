Case study

144 Archetypes. Idea to live in two weeks.

A personality test that pairs the 16 cognitive types with the nine Enneagram types, for 144 archetypes in all. We designed the brand and the site and built the web app: the test, sign-in, saved results and a paid full report.

What we made

  • The brand. A name that explains itself, and a mark of 16 outer rays around 9 inner ones, one for each type and each Enneagram number, set in a bold sans with an italic serif accent.
  • The test. 66 statements in 5 to 10 minutes, scored on both layers at once, so each person lands on one of the 144 archetypes.
  • Accounts and saved results. People sign in to keep their result and compare it later.
  • A paid full report. The test is free. The deeper report is paid through a FastSpring checkout that opens right on the page.
  • Reference pages. Every type and every Enneagram type has its own page, 25 in all, plus a page on why there are 144.

How we built it

We kept the first version to one job: take the test and get your archetype, with sign-in, saved results and one way to pay. It is built in Lovable on React, with Supabase for sign-in and saved results and FastSpring for the paid report. An AI-assisted builder wrote much of the code, so the time went into the test, the writing and the design, and it went live in about two weeks.
