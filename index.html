Questions. Straight answers before you sign up.

What do you build?

Web apps, websites and the design for all of it: screens and flows, new features, landing pages and decks, WordPress sites and plugins, and AI features like search, summaries or chat. iPhone apps on request.

Which option is right for me?

Most clients start with the monthly plan: design and development on anything, one request at a time. For work with a clear finish line, like a WordPress site or a new web app, we quote it as a custom project. Not sure? Book a free call and we'll tell you.

How does the monthly plan work?

Add requests to your board, as many as you like, and we work through them one at a time in the order you set. Most come back within 48 hours. Bigger work, like a new feature, is split into pieces, and you typically see an update every 48 hours. Design, development, fixes and AI features are all included. Pause or cancel at the end of any month.

How do custom projects work?

Tell us about it on a call, and we send one fixed quote based on the scope: a website, a WordPress build, a redesign, a plugin or a new web app. Bigger projects are paid half at the start and half on delivery, and you get 30 days of fixes after delivery.

Who will I work with?

The founder, Tim M., plans every project with you and stays your contact throughout. Developers we trust may join larger builds, and everything still runs through him.

Who owns the work?

You do. It's built in your own accounts for the code, the database and the hosting, so nothing is locked to us. Custom projects end with a short guide, a walkthrough video and 30 days of fixes.

How do I pay?

After a short call, we send you a secure link to pay. The monthly plan renews on its own until you pause or cancel. Bigger custom projects are paid half at the start and half on delivery.

Can I get a refund?

A month of the plan isn't refunded once it starts, because you can cancel at the end of any month. Custom projects have a fixed price agreed before we start. If something isn't working, tell us and we'll make it right.
