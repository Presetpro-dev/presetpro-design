Presetpro Design

Terms. The short version, then the details.

Last updated November 6, 2026

In short

  • The monthly plan: design and development for one flat fee, one request at a time.
  • Pause or cancel the plan at the end of any month.
  • Launch and custom projects: a fixed price and a date, agreed on one page first.
  • You set the order of the work on your board.
  • Everything we build is yours: code, accounts and design files.
  • Payment goes through FastSpring, our reseller.
  • A month isn't refunded once it starts, because you can stop at the end of any month.

Who we are

Presetpro Design is a design and build studio run by Presetpro INC, based in South Bruce Peninsula, Ontario, Canada. Tim M., the founder, leads every project and is your contact. We may bring in designers or developers we trust for parts of a project; when we do, they work under these terms and we stay responsible for their work. When these terms say "we" or "us", they mean Presetpro INC. When they say "you", they mean the person or company that hires us.

By joining the monthly plan, or booking a Launch or custom project, you agree to these terms. If anything is unclear, email contact@presetpro.com before you start.

The monthly plan

The monthly plan is design and development work for one flat monthly fee, at the price shown when you join. It covers new features, screens and flows, landing pages, decks, fixes and AI features, for an app or brand you already have or one we launched for you. The plan renews every month until you pause or cancel.

You add requests to your board, as many as you like, and we work on one at a time, in the order you set. Most requests come back within 48 hours, counted in business days. Bigger requests, like a new feature, are split into parts, and we typically share an update every 48 hours. These are typical times, not guarantees. If a request is still open when you stop, we hand over the work as it stands.

Launch projects

A Launch project is a fixed-price first version of a web app or an iPhone app. Before any work starts we agree a one-page scope with you: what the first version does, the screens it has, the price, how and when you pay, and the launch date. What is in that scope is what Launch includes.

New ideas that come up during the build are welcome. We note them and plan them for after launch, or quote them separately, so the price and the date hold.

We build in your own accounts: the code, the database, the hosting and, for iPhone apps, your own Apple developer account. Their fees are yours to pay, including Apple's yearly developer fee. Whether an app is accepted on the App Store is Apple's decision, so we can't promise it, but we fix anything Apple flags that falls within the scope.

For 30 days after launch we fix, at no charge, anything we built that doesn't work as the scope describes. After launch, you can keep building on the monthly plan.

Custom projects

A custom project is a piece of work with a clear finish line that we price on its own, like a website in WordPress, Webflow or Framer, a redesign or a plugin. After a call we agree a one-page scope with you: what we will make, the price, how and when you pay, and the delivery date. What is in that scope is what the project includes. New ideas that come up are planned for later or quoted separately.

We build in your own accounts, like your hosting and your WordPress site, and their fees are yours to pay. For 30 days after delivery we fix, at no charge, anything we built that doesn't work as the scope describes.

Payment

After a call, we send you a secure link to pay. Payments are handled by FastSpring, our authorized reseller and merchant of record. FastSpring takes the payment, adds any sales tax or VAT that applies where you are, and sends your receipts. Your card statement will show FastSpring. FastSpring's own terms also apply to the payment itself.

The monthly plan is charged automatically each month, to the card or account you paid with, until you pause or cancel. Launch and custom projects are paid as their scope says: usually half before we start and half on delivery.

If a monthly plan payment fails, work doesn't start until it goes through. Store discount codes don't apply to design and development work.

Your board

Features, fixes, ideas and design requests live on your private board, in the order you set. We work through them one at a time, top first. You can add, remove or reorder anything we haven't started yet.

Business days are Monday to Friday, not counting public holidays in Ontario.

Revisions

We revise each request until it's right, at no extra cost, then start the next one. A new direction or a new feature becomes a new request on your board.

What we design, and what we don't

We design and build web apps, and iPhone apps when a native app is the right next step, including the UX and UI and the brand pieces an app needs, like an icon or a landing page. Websites can be built in WordPress, Webflow or Framer. Android apps are planned on request.

We don't do 3D modeling, long animated videos, or print documents over 30 pages, like books or catalogs. If you are not sure, ask before you start.

Some things cost money on top of a project or the monthly plan, like hosting, domains, paid services, AI usage, font licenses and stock images. Those are yours to pay for, and we always ask before using anything that costs money.

Pausing

You can pause at the end of any month by telling us on your board or at contact@presetpro.com before your next billing date. The month you paid for runs to its end, then nothing is billed and we don't work on your board until you resume. There are no days to carry over. Your board, code and files stay as they are.

Cancelling and refunds

You can cancel any time from your billing page, or by telling us. Cancelling stops the next month of the plan. What you have already paid for runs to its end, and everything we made stays yours.

A month of the plan isn't refunded once it starts, because you can stop at the end of any month. Launch and custom projects are paid as their scope says. If something isn't working, tell us and we will make it right. Nothing here takes away any rights you have under the consumer laws where you live.

Who owns the work

Everything we deliver to you is yours. Once it is delivered and paid for, you own the code, the final designs and their source files, with full rights to use, change and sell them, and to register them as trademarks.

Two things stay with their owners: fonts, stock photos and other third-party assets, which come under their own licenses, and our own tools, templates and know-how, which we keep using for other work.

Showing the work

After your work is public, we may show it in our portfolio and on our channels. If you would rather we didn't, tell us and we won't. We are happy to sign an NDA when you need one.

Your materials and privacy

When you send us logos, photos, text or other material, you confirm you have the right to use it. We use it only for your work and keep anything that isn't public confidential.

How we handle personal information is explained in our Privacy Policy.

How we use AI

Every design is made and checked by a person. We may use AI tools for research and to speed up parts of the build, but never to produce final artwork on its own.

When an app we build for you includes AI features, they run on your own account with the AI provider, and that provider's fees and terms apply.

Liability

We do careful work, but we can't promise results like sales, sign-ups or search rankings. If something goes wrong, our total liability is limited to what you paid us in the three months before the claim, and we aren't responsible for indirect losses, like lost profits.

Changes to these terms

We may update these terms. If we make a change that affects clients, we will email you at least 14 days before it takes effect.

The law that applies

These terms are governed by the laws of Ontario and the federal laws of Canada that apply there.
